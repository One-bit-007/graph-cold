# Declaration of Generative AI and AI-Assisted Technologies in the Writing Process

During the preparation of this work the authors used generative AI assistants
(Kimi, Moonshot AI; GPT-based coding assistants) in order to assist with code
development and debugging of the experimental pipeline, and to assist with
language editing and drafting of the manuscript and submission documents.
After using these tools, the authors reviewed and edited the content as needed
and take full responsibility for the content of the publication.

The AI tools were not used to generate, alter, or analyze research data, and
they are not listed as authors. All experimental results, statistical claims,
and analyses were verified by the authors against the frozen experimental
artifacts described in the paper and its reproducibility package.
