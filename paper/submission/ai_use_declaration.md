# Declaration of Generative AI and AI-Assisted Technologies in the Writing Process

During the preparation of this work the authors used AI-assisted tools to
support code development and text drafting (including assistance with
implementation, editing, and language polishing). After using these tools, the
authors reviewed and edited the content as needed and take full responsibility
for the content of the publication.

No AI tool is listed as an author. All experimental results, analyses, and
claims were verified by the authors against the frozen experimental artifacts
described in the paper and its reproducibility documentation.
