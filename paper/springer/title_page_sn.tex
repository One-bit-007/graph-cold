\section*{Availability of data and materials}
All three datasets are publicly available from their original providers:
CIC-IDS-2017 from the Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity, University of New
Brunswick; CESNET-TLS-Year22 from Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.10608607); and
UNSW-NB15 from the University of New South Wales, Canberra. The derived
artifacts required to reproduce our results---code, configuration files, frozen
data-split manifests with content hashes, result tables, and figure-generation
scripts---are released in a public repository,
\url{https://github.com/One-bit-007/graph-cold}, and archived on Zenodo,
\url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21873516}.
