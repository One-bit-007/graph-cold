\subsection*{Availability of data and materials}
All three datasets are publicly available from their original providers:
CIC-IDS-2017 from the Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity, University of New
Brunswick; CESNET-TLS-Year22 from Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.10608607); and
UNSW-NB15 from the University of New South Wales, Canberra. The corrected
CICIDS-2017 labels used in the real-noise experiment are derived by applying
the publicly released labelling rules of Liu et al.\ (CNS 2022,
\url{https://github.com/GintsEngelen/CNS2022_Code}) to the original release.
The derived
artifacts required to reproduce our results---code, configuration files, frozen
data-split manifests with content hashes, the frozen noise-mapping matrix,
result tables, guard/regression tests, and table- and figure-generation
scripts---are released in a public repository,
\url{https://github.com/One-bit-007/graph-cold}, and archived on Zenodo,
\url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21873516}.
